What the ad should look like

The two characters, the set, the props and the caption style, drawn from the descriptions in the production pack.

These are my sketches, not Grok output. Grok will produce photorealistic people and a real-looking studio, so faces and details will differ. Use this to agree on the look before spending generations.

Characters

Quiet Mike

The pitchman. Loud, cheerful, completely sincere.

  • Stocky man, mid 40s, round friendly face
  • Short neat brown hair, clean shaven
  • Thick black-framed rectangular glasses
  • Mustard short-sleeved shirt, navy tie
  • Grey trousers, black belt

The Robot

A person in a homemade costume. Flat voice, stiff walk.

  • Silver-painted cardboard boxes with visible tape
  • Two round yellow lamp eyes, a slot mouth, one antenna
  • Silver dryer-hose arms and legs
  • Grey gloves and boots
  • Chest lamp: dark at first, green once plugged in

The set, as the camera sees it in every shot

Pale cream backdrop, flat bright lighting, a white table across the lower 40% of the frame, and a metal shelf of plain tubs on the right. The camera never moves. The jar, the black box and the socket are the only things that stay on the table.
