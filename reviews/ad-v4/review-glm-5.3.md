# Review: ad-v4

**All positions are in the 1920x1080 frame (frames supplied at 960x540, scaled x2). Safe caption canvas after the locked landing layout (page headline + Send button cover y 648-1080 and x 0-640): the wall band and right-side pockets, x 640-1920, y 0-640, avoiding Mike (x 700-1140), the tablet (x 780-1120, y 280-640, clips 4-6), the robot (x 1112-1340, clips 7-9), the blue/red wall art (x 1488-1860, y 0-240), and the tan set blob (x 1444-1640, y 300-540). Caption style per plan: heavy condensed caps, white fill, thick blue outline (sample the art panel blue, ~#3B5FA8), pop in 110% to 100%, 1-2px 90s jitter on impact, hard black offset shadow.**

## Verdict

The edit looks like the real thing: the orange 90s grade, locked tripod framing, matched cuts, and the composited app-on-tablet beats all hold up, and the set gives captions a native home (cream wall, blue/red art). The biggest problem: muted on the landing page this is a radio ad, and every proof point lives in a 160-pixel tablet nobody can read, while the physical gags land under the page's covered bottom-40%/left-third. The fix is entirely in post: wall-scale caption stamps, punch-in inserts on the three receipt beats, and one docs card in the agent section.

## Product clarity

- The plan's "stamped on the table" caption idea is dead on the landing page: the table top is exactly what the page overlays (y 648+). All story text must live on the wall band and right pockets; "next to props" survives (SEND. by the tablet, AN AGENT. on the robot).
- Bucket gag: the stamp must stay interrogative ("EVERY CHAIN?" plus the shrug). Do not add chain names or logos; the product sends USDC on Solana from a NEAR testnet account, nothing else.
- Hide mode: "HIDE WHO YOU PAID." is the correct scope; never private, anonymous, untraceable, or invisible. The amount and timing stay visible on screen; never blur the amount in any overlay or reframe.
- The receipt lines the app shows ("Not in this tx" HIDDEN, the two-step, "No transaction names you and them together.") are accurate and are the best copy in the edit; the punch-ins exist to make them legible, not to replace them.
- "ONLINE." at the green lamp is set dressing, not a product claim. Do not caption it "APPROVED."; approve_intent is only for over-limit intents held as NeedsApproval.
- Keep the "TESTNET." stamp up for its full 5 seconds; on a muted loop it is the only loud no-real-money signal. None of the on-screen text says private, anonymous, Ethereum, or mainnet, and none should.
- Docs card quotes only real doc lines: npm run mcp, policy.bibhu-usdc.testnet, per_transfer, daily, approve_intent, NeedsApproval. No "fully autonomous," no limit UI (those shots were dropped on purpose; the card names the limits without demoing them).
- Read as a sequence, the stamps carry the whole story muted: QUIETSEND, EVERY CHAIN?, NO NEW WALLET., ONE NEAR ACCOUNT. / SEND. / SENT!, HIDE WHO YOU PAID., YOU → VAULT → THEM, AN AGENT., PLUG IN YOUR AGENT / ONLINE., TESTNET. / TRY THIS AT HOME. That arc is the ad; nothing in it invents a feature.
